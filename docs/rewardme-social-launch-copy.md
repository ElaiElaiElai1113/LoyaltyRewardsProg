# RewardMe social launch copy

All pricing and reward claims remain subject to final owner and legal approval.

## General announcement

Support local. Earn more. RewardMe connects members with participating Filipino businesses so eligible everyday purchases can build toward meaningful rewards. Discover the network at https://rewardme-prod.vercel.app/

## Partner recruitment

Turn member visits into repeat relationships. RewardMe gives participating businesses a simple QR-based way to record eligible purchases, award rewards, and track activity. Final commission and settlement terms are confirmed in each partner agreement.

## Member education

One account. One member QR. Participating local businesses. Show your RewardMe QR before an eligible purchase is finalized, then follow your points and activity in one place.

## Safety note

RewardMe will never ask for your password, one-time code, or full payment-card details through social media.
