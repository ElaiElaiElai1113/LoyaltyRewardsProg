# RewardMe member quick-start

1. Create an account using your real name, email, and current phone or WhatsApp number.
2. Sign in and open **Member QR**.
3. At a participating business, show the QR before the sale is finalized.
4. Confirm that staff record the correct eligible purchase amount.
5. Check **Activity** for the recorded visit and awarded points.
6. Open **Rewards** or **Gift Cards** to view available uses.
7. Contact the monitored support address if a transaction is missing; include the business, date, amount, and receipt without sending passwords or full payment-card details.

Rewards depend on the participating offer and final member terms. They are not cash.
