# Pinas Rewards launch-day checklist

## Go/no-go

- [ ] Owner approved the commercial decision register.
- [ ] Philippine counsel approved all published legal documents.
- [ ] Working support and privacy contacts are monitored.
- [ ] Email authentication and delivery tests pass.
- [ ] Production health and database checks pass.
- [ ] Public, responsive, accessibility, and authenticated acceptance tests pass.
- [ ] A real phone successfully scans a member QR.
- [ ] At least one trained partner completes a test sale and reversal.
- [ ] Settlement owner and incident lead are named.
- [ ] Rollback owner and deployment reference are recorded.

## First-hour watch

- Check signups, sign-ins, QR transactions, rewards, email delivery, errors, and support every 15 minutes.
- Pause promotion if account access, tenant isolation, balance accuracy, privacy, or transaction integrity is uncertain.

## End-of-day

- Reconcile transactions and adjustments.
- Review support themes and incidents.
- Record metrics without exposing personal data.
- Decide whether to continue, pause, or roll back.
