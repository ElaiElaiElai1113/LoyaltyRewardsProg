# RewardMe staff QR guide

## Record a purchase

1. Open **Business Login** on the authorized device.
2. Select **Scan Member QR**.
3. Allow camera access, scan the member’s QR, and verify the displayed member.
4. Enter only the eligible purchase amount and invoice or receipt reference.
5. Review reward points and commission preview.
6. Submit once and wait for confirmation.

## If scanning fails

- Clean the camera and increase screen brightness.
- Ask the member to reopen the QR.
- Upload a QR screenshot or paste the member QR link only through the built-in fallback.
- Never photograph IDs, copy passwords, or store member QR images on a personal device.

## Correction

Do not submit a second transaction to “fix” the first. Record the transaction reference and ask the owner or support team to reverse or adjust it through the approved procedure.
