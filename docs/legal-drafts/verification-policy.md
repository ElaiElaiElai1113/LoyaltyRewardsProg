# RewardMe Verification Policy

Status: **DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW**

Verification is intended to protect accounts, limit duplicate participation, and reduce reward abuse. It must be proportionate to the action being protected.

## Launch position

Basic membership and the member QR currently do not require government ID during launch. Higher-risk actions may require additional checks only after the rule, user notice, data minimization, storage, review access, retention, rejection, and appeal process are approved.

## Information and review

Collect only information necessary for a documented purpose. Members must be told what is required, why it is required, who can review it, how long it is kept, and what happens if they decline.

## Decisions

Automated signals may assist review but should not be the sole basis for a significant adverse decision without an appropriate human-review path. Rejections should provide a useful reason and correction or appeal route where permitted.

## Security

Verification documents require restricted access, encrypted transport and storage, audit logs, retention limits, secure deletion, and an incident-response plan.
