## Information processed

RewardMe may process account and contact details, program membership, transaction and reward history, referrals, device and security events, customer-support communications, consent records, and optional verification information. The final notice must list every production data field and vendor.

## Purposes and legal bases

Purposes include providing accounts and rewards, recording purchases, preventing abuse, customer support, enforcing agreements, complying with law, and—only with appropriate consent—marketing. Counsel must map each purpose to an applicable lawful basis.

## Sharing

Information may be shared with participating businesses when needed to validate a purchase or redemption, and with contracted service providers such as hosting, database, email, monitoring, and payment providers. RewardMe must not sell personal data. A final processor and cross-border transfer register is required.

## Retention and security

Retention periods must be documented by data category. Access controls, encryption, backups, incident response, audit logging, and deletion workflows must be validated before launch. Government ID data, if used, should be minimized and retained only as long as necessary.

## Rights

The final notice must explain applicable rights to be informed, access, object, correct, erase or block, data portability, complain, and seek damages, including identity-verification and response procedures.
