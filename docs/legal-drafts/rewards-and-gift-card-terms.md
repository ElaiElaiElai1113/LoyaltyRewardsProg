# Pinas Rewards Rewards and Gift Card Terms

Status: **DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW**

## Reward points and offers

Points are an accounting measure used to access eligible offers. They are not legal tender, bank deposits, securities, or a promise of cash. The final conversion method, earning rules, exclusions, expiration, caps, and rounding rules must be displayed before members transact.

## Redemption

Redemptions are subject to membership status, available balance, offer inventory, participating locations, dates, and any stated verification step. A completed redemption cannot be reversed except where required by law or expressly allowed by the offer.

## Refunds and reversals

Refunded or cancelled purchases may reverse related points or benefits. If a balance becomes negative, future earnings may first restore it, subject to counsel approval and clear member notice.

## Gift cards

Before issuing gift cards, the owner and counsel must confirm issuer identity, validity period, fees, transferability, replacement of lost codes, partial redemption, refunds, unclaimed balances, and whether Philippine payment or stored-value rules apply. Gift-card marketing must not launch while these fields remain unresolved.

## Fraud and errors

Pinas Rewards may pause suspicious activity and correct duplicate or erroneous transactions after review. Members must have a documented support and appeal route.
