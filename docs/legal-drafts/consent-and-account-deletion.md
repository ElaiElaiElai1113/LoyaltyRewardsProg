# Pinas Rewards Consent and Account Deletion Notice

Status: **DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW**

## Separate consent choices

Required acceptance of service terms must be separate from optional marketing consent. Marketing consent should be specific, informed, freely given, recorded with timestamp and policy version, and easy to withdraw.

Suggested unchecked option:

> I agree to receive Pinas Rewards launch news and member offers by email. I can unsubscribe at any time.

Do not bundle partner marketing, SMS, profiling, or sharing with unrelated companies into this choice.

## Account deletion

Members should be able to request deletion through a documented in-app or support process. Before deletion, explain identity confirmation, pending transactions, membership cancellation, remaining rewards, legally required retention, de-identification, backup handling, and expected completion time.

## Operational workflow

1. Record and acknowledge the request.
2. Verify identity proportionately.
3. Restrict optional processing while reviewing.
4. Resolve legal or financial retention requirements.
5. Delete or de-identify eligible data across production systems and processors.
6. Confirm completion without exposing sensitive details.
