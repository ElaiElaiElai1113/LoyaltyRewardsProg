# RewardMe Savings Plan Supplement

Status: **DRAFT — FEATURE NOT LIVE — SPECIALIST LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED**

RewardMe must not accept savings-plan locks or promise a bonus or payout until
counsel confirms the legal classification of the final model and the owner has
approved its funding and operational rules.

## Required disclosures

The final supplement must state that RewardMe reward units are not bank
deposits, legal tender, securities, or government-insured funds unless counsel
determines otherwise. It must precisely describe eligible reward sources,
minimum and maximum amounts, lock period, maturity, bonus formula, payout form,
funding source, fees, taxes, expiry, and account-termination treatment.

## Early release and hardship

The owner and counsel must decide whether early release is prohibited, allowed
without a bonus, allowed with a fee or forfeiture, or available for defined
hardship events. Members must see the consequence before confirming a lock.

## Records and complaints

Members should receive a durable confirmation and ledger for every lock,
adjustment, release, bonus, and payout. Corrections require an audit trail.
Support, complaint, dispute, reconciliation, insolvency, and incident handling
must be documented before launch.
