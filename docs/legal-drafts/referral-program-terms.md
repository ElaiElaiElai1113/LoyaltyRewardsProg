# RewardMe Referral Program Terms

Status: **DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW**

## Eligibility and qualifying action

The final terms must define who may refer, who qualifies as a new member, the
attribution window, the action that earns an award, and whether paid membership
must remain active for a minimum period. Self-referrals, duplicate accounts,
fabricated purchases, and automated or misleading promotion should be prohibited.

## Proposed awards

The pitch proposes $10 in rewards for an eligible Regular referral and separate
Gold conversion rewards. These values are not payable until the owner approves
the exact Gold rule, currency treatment, caps, funding, expiry, and reversal.

## Attribution, caps and reversals

The final terms must explain which referral wins when multiple links or codes
are used, maximum awards per period, excluded relationships or channels, and
what happens after cancellation, refund, chargeback, fraud, or account deletion.

## Disclosure and disputes

Referrers must clearly disclose that they may receive value. RewardMe should
provide a documented review and appeal route and reserve proportionate rights
to pause or reverse suspicious awards after investigation.
