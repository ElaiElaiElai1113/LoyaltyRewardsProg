# RewardMe support playbook

## Required ticket fields

- Requester name and account email
- Category and urgency
- Business, transaction date, amount, and reference where relevant
- Clear description and expected result
- Consent-safe attachments only

Never request passwords, one-time codes, full card numbers, or unnecessary ID copies.

## Response targets

Proposed until owner approval: acknowledge account/security issues within four business hours and ordinary questions within one business day. Publish actual support hours with the working mailbox.

## Standard replies

**Missing transaction:** “Thanks for reporting this. Please send the participating business, purchase date, amount, and receipt reference. Do not send payment-card details.”

**Account access:** “We can help secure the account. Please use the password-reset link. RewardMe will never ask for your password or one-time code.”

**Reward eligibility:** “Rates and eligibility follow the offer shown for the participating business. We’ll review the recorded transaction and explain any adjustment.”

**Privacy request:** “We’ve recorded your privacy request and will verify your identity using the minimum information necessary before processing it.”

Escalate security, privacy, payment, legal threats, and widespread outages immediately.
