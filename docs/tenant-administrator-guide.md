# Tenant administrator guide

## Branding

Maintain the program name, logo, colors, locale, currency, timezone, support contact, legal agreements, map region, and email identity. Preview public and email surfaces before publishing changes.

## Domains

Use the platform subdomain until ownership is verified. Domain activation requires DNS ownership, HTTPS, health checks, authentication redirects, and sender-domain verification.

## Team access

Invite only the required administrators, business owners, and staff. Review pending invitations and remove access promptly when responsibilities change.

## Data export

Request tenant and audit exports through the platform operations process. Verify the SHA-256 manifest, store exports outside source control, and use reconciliation reports for migrations or disputes.
