# Rewards Platform store submission materials

## Shared listing draft

**Name:** Rewards Platform

**Short description:** Join local rewards programs, earn program-specific value, and manage benefits from one secure account.

**Full description:** Rewards Platform connects members with participating local businesses across independently operated rewards programs. Members can join eligible programs, view their program-specific balance and activity, discover participating businesses, access rewards and gift cards, manage agreements, and present their member QR code. Balances and activity remain separate between programs.

**Keywords:** local rewards, loyalty, member benefits, businesses, gift cards

**Support category:** Lifestyle / Shopping

## Privacy disclosure draft

The application processes account identity, contact details, program membership, transactions, agreements, rewards balances, referrals, gift cards, and diagnostic information needed to provide and secure the service. Final Apple privacy labels and Google Play data-safety answers must match the production analytics, email, crash-reporting, and payment providers.
